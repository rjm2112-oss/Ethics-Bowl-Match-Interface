# Approved final scoring format

Each independent high-reasoning judge receives both full cases and moderator questions, presenter identities and the complete delivered match discussion with the unchanged official 2025 rubric. Judges never receive other judges’ cards. Pending/unresolved delivery blocks scoring; run/transcript guards discard stale results.

The strict new response has only participantOne and participantTwo. Each contains seven integer scores (5/5/5/10/20/10/5) plus didWell and needsImprovement. Each judge supplies “What the team did well” and “What needs improvement” separately for EACH participant, not one overall comment and not per-criterion prose. The app validates bounds and required nonblank fields, calculates /60 totals and tallies votes.

Human/hybrid scoring requires the same seven bounded integer scores per participant, computed totals and four comment fields per judge. Setup persistence restores criteria and paired comments. Historical completed cards retain their original single comment without fabricating fields.

Completed cards publish incrementally in dialogue with a compact score table and paired participant comments. Cards are deduplicated and never sent to TTS, manually read aloud or included as substantive discussion in later judge requests. The final winner announcement remains separate.

Numeric/schema, independent-scoring, deduplication, incremental display, human/hybrid entry, reload, cancellation and zero-TTS behavior are covered by mocked tests. Native offline views pass desktop/iPad. No new paid calls or builds accompany this format.
